## 1. What the app is and isn't

### What it is
A journal for inner experiences (dreams, meditations, mystical experiences, somatic states, creative inspiration, and similar) augmented with AI assistance for noticing patterns across your captures.

### What it isn't
- **Not therapy.** The app does not provide medical, psychological, or psychiatric care. It is not a substitute for a licensed mental-health professional.
- **Not crisis support.** If you are in a mental-health crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (988 in the United States; see <https://findahelpline.com> for international options). Do not rely on the app for emergencies.

## 2. AI-generated content

When you request a reading, synthesis, or other AI-assisted output:

- The response is generated by Anthropic's Claude model.
- It reflects the content you provided plus general patterns the model has been trained on.
- It is **not infallible.** Claude can be wrong, misleading, or simply off-key for your specific situation.
- The Lantern's voice is poetic by design. Treat its observations as invitations to your own attention — not authoritative claims about you.
- You decide what is true for you. The app's job is to widen the aperture of your sight; the seeing is yours.

## 4. Connecting with others (Go Out — the Weave)

Go Out is the app's only feature that involves another person, and it is entirely optional. When you use it, you may share parts of your experiences — up to and including an experience as you recorded it — and exchange messages with people you have deliberately connected with.

**Because other people receive what you share, there is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior.** This includes harassment, threats, hate, sexual content directed at another person without their clear consent, any sexualization of minors, impersonation, and using the Weave to monitor, pressure, or intimidate someone. There is no warning system for this category: violations can result in removal of shared content, blocked connections, and immediate, permanent loss of your account.

**Moderation.** Every synchronicity and connection has a Report action. A person — not an automated system — reviews every report, and we act on legitimate reports within 3 business days, including removing content and ejecting the user who provided it. Blocking someone stops all matching and sharing with them immediately.

**What you share is delivered.** A message or shared experience that has reached another person's device cannot be recalled. If what you share names another person, you are responsible for sharing it with care.

## 5. AI usage limits

To keep the service sustainable for all users, AI features are rate-limited:

- **Readings + recasts:** 10 per day in your first two weeks, 5 per day in week three, 3 per day thereafter. The lower long-term cap reflects sustainable patterns of inner work — most weeks you won't need more than that.
- **Synthesis regenerations:** count against the same daily pool.

Limits reset at 00:00 UTC each day. If you hit a limit, the Lantern will simply ask you to read tomorrow.
