When you request a reading, synthesis, or other AI-assisted output:

- The response is generated by Anthropic's Claude model.
- It reflects the content you provided plus general patterns the model has been trained on.
- It is **not infallible.** Claude can be wrong, misleading, or simply off-key for your specific situation.
- The Lantern's voice is poetic by design. Treat its observations as invitations to your own attention — not authoritative claims about you.

## 4. AI usage limits

To keep the service sustainable for all users, AI features are rate-limited:

- **Readings + recasts:** 10 per day in your first two weeks, 5 per day in week three, 3 per day thereafter. The lower long-term cap reflects sustainable patterns of inner work — most weeks you won't need more than that.
- **Synthesis regenerations:** count against the same daily pool.

Limits reset at 00:00 UTC each day. If you hit a limit, the Lantern will simply ask you to read tomorrow.
